Uncompromising Privacy

How Secure Is Your Call?

We believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Here is our architectural guarantee.

End-to-End Encryption

WebRTC enforces mandatory DTLS (Datagram Transport Layer Security) and SRTP (Secure Real-time Transport Protocol). Media packets are encrypted with AES ciphers directly on your device before transmission.

Server-Bypass Media Stream

Our server acts solely as a signaling mediator to exchange connection descriptors. Once connected, video and audio streams flow strictly between you and your peer. The server never handles your video or audio frames.

Zero Logs & Zero Recordings

We do not record calls, store chat transcripts, track IP logs, or keep participant histories. When you close the browser tab, the room memory disappears completely.

Zero Personal Data Collection

No user profiling, no cookies tracking across websites, no telemetry harvesting, and no mandatory app permissions outside camera/mic access during active calls.
